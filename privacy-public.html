Privacy Policy

Updated 23 October 2026

What we store

Staff logins (email and a hashed password) and their roles. Customer details such as name, address, phone number and personal ID, and the accounts, cards, loans and transactions linked to them.

Who can see it

Only logged-in staff. Cashiers see customers and accounts; admins manage users. The public home page shows totals only.

Cookies

One cookie keeps staff logged in. The light or dark appearance choice is saved in the browser only.
